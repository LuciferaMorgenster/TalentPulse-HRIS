## 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA) Capabilities

TalentPulse is fully PWA-enabled:
- **Instant Installation**: Click the **Install App** button in the header bar on Chrome, Edge, Android, or use **Add to Home Screen** on iOS Safari.
- **Offline Reliability**: Powered by `vite-plugin-pwa` and Service Worker precaching (`workbox`). The application loads instantly even without an active internet connection, serving cached assets and retaining local personnel dossiers.
- **Native Look & Feel**: Runs in standalone window mode with customized system theme colors (`#2563eb`), high-resolution brand icons (192x192, 512x512, maskable with safe-zone margin), and an offline connectivity indicator.

## 🇬🇧 English Overview

**TalentPulse** is a modern, responsive, and multilingual Human Resources Information System (HRIS / Personnel Information Management) built for high-performing teams, scaling SMEs, and distributed companies. It centralizes employee records, leave administration, contract lifecycles, and performance evaluations while offering automated data synchronisation with financial and project tools.

Live Demo: [https://talentpulse-hris.lucifera.eu](https://talentpulse-hris.lucifera.eu)

### Key Capabilities
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable directly on desktop and mobile devices with full offline support.
- **Comprehensive Employee Directory**: Rich employee profiles including role, department, contract status, contact details, skills matrix, tax/social security identifiers, and assigned assets.
- **Leave & Absence Workflow**: Interactive PTO calendar, categorized requests (Vacation, Sick, Parental, Unpaid), automated day balance calculation, and manager approval queues.
- **Contracts & Compensation**: Salary figures, payment periods, working hours/week, renewal dates, and contract document metadata.
- **BoekhoudGemak & Kopilot Project Bridges**:
  - **BoekhoudGemak**: Synchronizes employee master data with payroll ledgers, IBAN accounts, and fiscal compliance.
  - **Kopilot Project**: Connects personnel skill sets and capacity with real-time project task allocation and billable hours.
- **Instant 5-Language Switching**: English (default), Nederlands (Dutch), Deutsch (German), Français (French), and Español (Spanish).
- **SEO & Social Share Ready**: Pre-configured OpenGraph and Twitter Cards optimized for LinkedIn and Facebook sharing with custom 1200x630 visual preview assets.

## 🇳🇱 Nederlandse Beschrijving

**TalentPulse** is een geavanceerde, meertalige applicatie voor personeelsinformatiebeheer (HRIS). De software stroomlijnt alle personeelsprocessen: van werving en contractbeheer tot verlofaanvragen en prestatiebeoordelingen.

Live Versie: [https://talentpulse-hris.lucifera.eu](https://talentpulse-hris.lucifera.eu)

### Belangrijkste Functies
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installeerbaar als native applicatie op desktop, Android en iOS, inclusief offlinemodus via Service Workers.
- **Centraal Personeelsdossier**: Beheer alle gegevens van medewerkers overzichtelijk op één plek (functie, afdeling, contactgegevens, vaardigheden, IBAN en BSN/fiscaal nummer).
- **Verlof- en Verzuimregistratie**: Duidelijke verlofkalender, automatische berekening van verlofdagen, statusbeoordeling (Goedgekeurd, In afwachting, Afgewezen).
- **Contract- & Salarisoverzicht**: Inzicht in bruto salarissen, uren per week, contracttypes en naderende verlengingsdata.
- **Koppeling met BoekhoudGemak & Kopilot Project**:
  - **BoekhoudGemak**: Automatische synchronisatie van personeelsdossiers naar de salarisadministratie en grootboekrekeningen.
  - **Kopilot Project**: Directe toewijzing van medewerkers aan projecten op basis van beschikbaarheid en competenties.
- **Volledig Meertalig**: Schakel met één klik tussen Nederlands, Engels (standaard), Duits, Frans en Spaans.
- **Geoptimaliseerd voor LinkedIn & Facebook**: Volledige OpenGraph en Twitter Card ondersteuning met professionele preview-omslagafbeelding.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **PWA & Offline**: `vite-plugin-pwa`, Web App Manifest, Service Worker Precaching (`workbox`)
- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite 8, Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons & Motion**: Lucide React, Motion
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans & DM Sans (Google Fonts)
- **Metadata**: Schema.org WebApplication JSON-LD, OpenGraph (1200x630), Twitter Summary Large Card
- **Assets**: High-resolution generated cover image stored in `public/cover.jpg` and compliant PWA PNG/SVG icons
